# Industry / Niche Strategy



**Project:** Softabyte Labs  

**Phase:** 0 — Planning only (revised Phase 3.2 — multi-industry architecture; expanded Phase 5A)  

**Date:** August 19, 2026 (Phase 5A research: August 20, 2026)



Softabyte Labs serves businesses **across multiple industries**. Ecommerce is an important vertical, not the only one.



Related documents: `service_architecture.md`, `keyword_map.md`, `website_architecture.md`, `industry_seo_research.md`



---



## 1. Non-negotiable architecture rule



Do **not** create:



- `/services/ecommerce-development/`

- `/industries/ecommerce-business/`

- `/industries/ecommerce-solutions/`

- `/industries/ecommerce-companies/`



Industries are **not** core services. Each industry gets **one** canonical URL when the page can be unique.



---



## 2. How industry pages differ from service pages



| Service page intent | Industry page intent |

|---|---|

| “I need this capability.” | “I operate in this market and need a partner who understands my operating reality.” |

| Organized by what we build | Organized by buyer problems in that vertical |

| Examples may mention industries | Services appear as solutions to those problems |



If an industry page only restates the six services with the industry name swapped in, it is a doorway page and must not be published.



---



## 3. Industry hub



**URL:** `/industries/`  

**Role:** Explain the vertical approach and route to industry pages with distinct intent.  

**Do not** publish thin pages simply to target “software development company for [industry].”

## 5. Ecommerce & Retail (primary vertical — keyword mapped)



**URL:** `/industries/ecommerce/`  

**Primary keyword:** ecommerce software development  

**Recommended H1:** Technology for ecommerce businesses, not another theme store  

**Intent:** Commercial/industry — operators who need software, UX, apps, automation, and reliable infrastructure for commerce  



### Business problems this page should own



- Checkout, catalog, and merchandising friction

- Operations split across storefront, warehouse, support, and ads

- Mobile shopping and branded apps

- Inventory, order, and customer data that do not talk to each other

- Manual work that should be automated

- Slow or fragile storefronts and hosting



### Cannibalization control



| Page | May say | Must not own |

|---|---|---|

| Web Development | We build ecommerce storefronts and web apps | The operating model of an ecommerce company |

| Custom Software | Commerce-adjacent systems and integrations | “Ecommerce development” as the H1 |

| Ecommerce industry | The full operator picture | Recreating each service page |

## 6. Other approved industries (Phase 5A — keyword mapped, pages not built)



Each page must meet the template in section 8 before publication. Full research: `industry_seo_research.md`.



| Industry | Primary keyword | Confidence | SEO role |

|---|---|---|---|

| Healthcare | healthcare software development | MEDIUM | Commercial vertical — publish after trust assets; YMYL caution |

| Transportation & Logistics | logistics software development | MEDIUM-HIGH | Commercial vertical — logistics-first copy under combined display name |

| Professional Services | software for professional services firms | LOW (SEO) / MEDIUM (IA) | Navigation + positioning; moderate organic expectations |

| Startups & SaaS | SaaS development company | MEDIUM-HIGH | Commercial vertical — combined page; SaaS-first, startup/MVP secondary |



**Do not publish** any industry page until unique, honest content can be written.



### Healthcare (Phase 5A)

**URL:** `/industries/healthcare/`  

**H1 direction:** Technology built around modern healthcare operations  

**Intent:** Commercial — organizations needing patient/staff portals, admin workflows, integrations, and secure engineering without unverified compliance claims.



**Must not claim:** HIPAA certification, SOC 2, HITRUST, FDA SaMD expertise, clinical outcomes, or named EHR integration credentials unless verified.



### Real Estate (Phase 5A)

**URL:** `/industries/real-estate/`  

**H1 direction:** Software for property businesses where data and transactions never sit still  

**Intent:** Commercial — brokerages, property managers, PropTech founders needing portals, transaction workflows, and property ops systems.



**Must not invent:** MLS/IDX integration expertise or RESO certification unless supported.



### Transportation & Logistics (Phase 5A)

**URL:** `/industries/transportation-logistics/`  

**H1 direction:** Software for logistics businesses where operations cannot stand still  

**Intent:** Commercial — carriers, brokers, and operators needing dispatch, fleet, visibility, and ops software.



**Primary keyword uses “logistics”** while display name keeps Transportation & Logistics.



### Professional Services (Phase 5A)

**URL:** `/industries/professional-services/`  

**H1 direction:** Technology for firms that sell expertise, not shelf products  

**Intent:** Commercial/navigation — consultancies, agencies, and knowledge firms needing client portals and workflow tools. SERPs for exact-match phrases are dominated by PSA/ERP products; do not force SEO where intent is weak.



**Must not imply:** legal or accounting regulatory specialization without evidence.



### Startups & SaaS (Phase 5A)

**URL:** `/industries/startups-saas/`  

**H1 direction:** Product engineering for SaaS teams building something worth scaling  

**Intent:** Commercial — SaaS product companies and founders needing MVP-to-scale engineering.



**Keep combined page.** Do not split into separate startup and SaaS URLs without approval. Must not cannibalize Custom Software or Web Development primaries.

## 7. Audience vs industry



**Startups, SMBs, mid-market, and growing companies** remain audiences addressed on homepage, About, and service pages.



**Startups & SaaS** as an industry page is allowed when content focuses on **product/SaaS operating realities** — not a generic “we help startups” page that duplicates every service.



---



## 8. Industry page template (required before publication)



Every industry page must include:



- Industry-specific operating problems

- Which of the six services apply and why

- What we will not claim

- Links to services, work, relevant articles, contact

- Unique H1 and unique primary keyword



If those cannot be written honestly, the page waits.



---



## 9. SEO guardrail



Avoid doorway-page architecture:



- No six thin pages targeting “software development company for [industry]”

- No fake statistics, client logos, or certification claims

- No duplicating service page content with an industry name inserted



See `keyword_map.md` and `industry_seo_research.md` for full keyword ownership, cannibalization guardrails, and publication order.
