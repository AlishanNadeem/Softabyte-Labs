**Project:** Softabyte Labs  
**Phase:** 0 — Planning only  
**Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Status:** **FROZEN**

Covers US voice, blog quality, topical clusters, and the first 90 days of content. CMS/admin is designed later; this is SEO strategy only.

Related: `keyword_strategy.md`, `internal_linking_strategy.md`, `conversion_strategy.md`

---

## 1. US content guidelines

**Tone:** professional, confident, clear, human, modern, business-focused, technically credible, conversion-focused.

**Write like a capable US partner**, not like a brochure from a staffing mill.

Use:

- Direct sentences
- Specific problems
- Honest limits (“this is the wrong fit if…”)
- US spelling (standardize: ecommerce, automate, optimize)

Avoid:

- “We are the best,” “cutting-edge synergy,” “end-to-end holistic solutions”
- Fake clients, awards, testimonials, certifications, statistics
- Keyword stuffing
- Generic AI filler and repetitive outlines
- Search-engine-first writing that no buyer would forward to a colleague

If a proof point cannot be sourced, omit it.

---

## 2. Blog role

The blog exists to:

- Support commercial service and industry pages
- Answer mid-funnel questions
- Earn topical authority
- Send qualified readers to Contact

It must **not** become an AI content farm.

Launch rule: **fewer articles, fully finished**, rather than a calendar of 40 thin posts.

---

## 3. Quality bar for every article

Each post must have:

- A real search intent
- A primary keyword that does not steal a service page primary
- A human outline before drafting
- Original examples or reasoning
- Internal links per `internal_linking_strategy.md`
- A single conversion opportunity
- An author (even if one studio author at first)

Drafts never go public. Published posts are indexable.

## 7. Blog and admin (frozen — Phase 0)

**Approved architecture:**

- Blog posts = MongoDB (`blog_posts`)
- Blog management = Admin Panel
- Blog publishing = Admin Panel
- Draft/published status = MongoDB

**Do not use MDX as the production blog CMS.**

Static marketing pages remain code-managed. The admin panel exists so SEO-optimized blog content can be published and managed without developer deploys for every article.

Blog records use the fields defined in `dynamic_vs_static_architecture.md` (`title`, `slug`, `excerpt`, `content`, SEO fields, `status`, `published_at`, etc.), all `snake_case`.

Public blog posts must be quality-gated and indexable **only when published**.
