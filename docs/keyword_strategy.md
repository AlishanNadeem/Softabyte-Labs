# Keyword Strategy

**Project:** Softabyte Labs  
**Phase:** 0 — Planning only  
**Market:** United States  
**Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Status:** **FROZEN**

**Data policy:** No search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, traffic, or ranking numbers are listed here because no reliable keyword-tool export was used. Priorities are **qualitative**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW.

**Basis for qualitative scores:**

- Observed commercial landing pages across US-selling agencies (August 2026)
- Query language (hire / company / agency / cost / vs = stronger commercial intent)
- Fit to Softabyte Labs’ six services and Ecommerce niche
- Ability to write unique, honest content
- Cannibalization risk

If a third-party dataset is added later, record **tool, date, market = United States**.

---

## 1. Intent model

| Class | Meaning | Site role |
|---|---|---|
| A. Commercial | Comparing providers or evaluating a capability to buy | Service, industry, hub pages |
| B. Transactional | Ready to contact, hire, or start | Contact, strong CTAs on money pages |
| C. Informational | Learning, scoping, de-risking | Blog, Process, parts of service FAQs |
| D. Navigational | Looking for Softabyte Labs by name | Homepage, brand SERP later |

---

## 1a. Homepage keyword role (frozen)

The homepage is **not** a high-intent service landing page. It primarily supports:

1. **Brand / navigational** — Softabyte Labs
2. **Broad relevance** — software, product, and technology partner positioning
3. **US market commercial positioning** — technology partner for US businesses

**Dedicated service pages** remain responsible for high-intent primaries:

- custom software development company
- web development company
- mobile app development company
- AI automation for business
- UI UX design agency
- managed application hosting

Homepage copy, H1, and body text must remain **natural**. Do not force exact-match keyword phrases into the homepage simply because they appear in planning documents.

See `keyword_map.md` for the homepage entry.

---

## 2. What we will not target as primary keywords

- “Best software company in the world”
- Award and directory keywords as page-1 strategy
- Every AI buzzword as its own URL
- “Cheap website,” “offshore developers,” “hire dedicated team in 48 hours”
- City doorway keywords without a real local presence
- Ecommerce-as-service primaries on a `/services/` URL

## 12. Prioritization rules

1. Business relevance beats raw popularity.
2. If two terms share intent, they share a URL.
3. Informational terms support pillars; they do not become extra service pages.
4. US buyer language: company, agency, hire, process, cost, timeline — not “outsource dedicated resources.”
5. Do not assign the same primary keyword to two pages.
